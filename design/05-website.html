Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need an app?

No. Most phones read NFC on their own: iPhone XS and newer, and most Android phones with NFC turned on. Anyone else can scan the QR code with their camera.

Can I change where a card goes later?

Tap cards open the link you give us, and the chip is locked after programming so nobody else can change it. If you'd like to change where your card goes later, choose a website package, where the card opens a page we manage for you.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, brand colors if you have them, and the links for each card: your Google review page, social pages, or your menu. We'll handle the rest.

What's the yearly fee on website packages for?

It keeps your site online: hosting, security updates, and up to 2 small edit requests a month, like new prices, photos or hours. Bigger changes are quoted first. Your first year is included in the package price.

Do I need my own domain?

It's optional. Launch Kit and Custom package can use your own domain, like yourcafe.ph. If you want one and don't have it yet, we'll help you register it and bill you the domain's actual price, added to your first-year price and to your yearly fee after that. Tap Page lives on your Tappd link, so no domain is needed.

How long does it take?

Orders of up to 4 cards are ready in same day to 2 days, once we have your logo and links. Larger orders and website packages depend on the size of the job and how quickly we get your content. We'll confirm the timeline when you order.

What if my card is faulty?

If a card is defective or won't read when you receive it, we replace it free. Message us and we'll sort it out.

How do I get my order?

We'll confirm pickup or delivery, and any shipping fee, when you order.

Will it work on a metal counter?

Metal can block NFC. We'll recommend a placement or a stand, and the QR code always works as a backup.
